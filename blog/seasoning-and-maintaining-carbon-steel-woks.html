Seasoning and Maintaining Carbon Steel Woks for Lifetime Cooking

By Wok Restoration Specialist • 8 Min Read

A seasoned carbon steel wok is an extension of the chef's hand. **High-Heat Oil Polymerization** forms a smooth black non-stick patina that resists food sticking without toxic chemical coatings like Teflon. This step-by-step guide details carbon steel wok care.

1. Initial Blueing Heat Treatment

Heating raw carbon steel over open flames until it turns dark iridescent blue removes factory oils and opens metal pores.

2. Oil Polymerization & Scallion Aromatics

Stir-frying ginger and scallions in high-smoke-point oil coats the interior with a natural slick patina that deepens with every cook.
