Mastering the Art of High-Heat Wok Hei Cooking

By Master Wok Chef • 8 Min Read

Wok Hei, literally translated as "Breath of the Wok", is the elusive complex smoky aroma that defines authentic Chinese wok cooking. **1000°F Jet Burner Flame Searing** atomizes microscopic oil droplets inside the wok, creating rapid Maillard caramelization without soggy steam buildup. This guide details Wok Hei physics.

1. Microscopic Oil Droplet Vaporization

When food is tossed high above a 1000°F jet flame, aerosolized oil droplets ignite briefly, imparting a distinctive smoky note.

2. Carbon Steel Heat Transfer Physics

Unlike stainless steel, seasoned carbon steel transfers intense thermal energy instantly, searing meats in under 45 seconds.

"Wok Hei is not a flavor you add; it is a thermal reaction you capture with speed and high flame."
