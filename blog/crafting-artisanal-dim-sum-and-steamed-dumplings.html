Crafting Artisanal Dim Sum and Steamed Bamboo Dumplings

By Dim Sum Master Chef • 8 Min Read

Traditional dim sum is an intricate art form where delicate dough skins seal in savory juices and seasoned fillings. **Hand-Pleated Bamboo Dumpling Steaming** circulates moist heat gently, cooking wild shrimp and organic Berkshire pork to tender perfection. This guide details dim sum craftsmanship.

1. The 12-Pleat Dumpling Sealing Technique

Master dim sum chefs hand-pleat dumpling edges 12 times to seal in rich broths without tearing thin dough skins.

2. Natural Bamboo Steamer Steam Dynamics

Bamboo wood absorbs excess condensation during steaming, preventing dumplings from becoming soggy on top.
