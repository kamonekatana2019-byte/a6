Artisanal Chili Crisp and Garlic Chili Oil Reductions

By Sauce Atelier Chef • 8 Min Read

The secret weapon of street food flavor is a spoonful of crunchy, aromatic chili crisp. **Artisanal Slow-Infused Chili Oil** combines numbing Sichuan peppercorns, roasted garlic chips, crispy shallots, and star anise in hot peanut oil. This guide details chili crisp craft.

1. Controlled Temperature Oil Infusion

Infusing whole aromatics at 250°F extracts essential oils without scorching delicate chili flakes or garlic chips.

2. Sichuan Peppercorn Numbing Balance

Toasting red and green Sichuan peppercorns adds a citrusy, numbing sensation (Mala) that balances savory noodle dishes.
