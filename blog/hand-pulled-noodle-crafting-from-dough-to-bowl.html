Hand-Pulled Noodle Crafting: From Organic Wheat Dough to Bowl

By Noodle Master • 8 Min Read

Hand-pulled noodles (Lamian) possess a unique elastic bounce and toothsome chew (Q texture) that machine-extruded noodles cannot replicate. **Gluten Matrix Stretching & Fresh Pulling** transforms organic wheat dough into dozens of uniform strands in seconds. This guide explores hand-pulled noodle techniques.

1. Wheat Dough Resting & Alkaline Salt Balance

Blending high-protein organic wheat flour with alkaline mineral salts relaxes gluten strands for infinite stretchability.

2. Flash Boiling for Springy Q Elasticity

Boiling freshly pulled strands for just 60 seconds in rolling cauldrons locks in springy chew before serving in rich broths.
